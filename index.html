The Track

  • Roughly 3 miles. Ski runs and fire roads. Near 8,000 ft
  • MX section with jumps and berms. Finish line jump under an SX-style overhead truss
  • Fout's Forest · singletrack section, longer this year. Built by MXA test rider Fout (@atchafout), named for him
  • New for 2026: shipping container jump in the base area, slowed-down start, faster sections, built-up berms, pro-only section designed for viewing
  • Snowmaking equipment every 100 ft waters the course. Dust stays down
  • Steep and abrupt elevation. There are rocks. Promoters warned riders directly
  • This is an off-road race, not an MX race. Skid plates and mousse or heavy-duty tubes recommended

Chairlift and Summit

Saturday only. Free with facility wristband.

  • Chair 5. Mountain waiver required
  • Riding up you're looking straight down at the course
  • Short walk down to a set viewing area
  • BC Goggle Co bringing shade and seating
  • Light food, beverage, bathrooms up top
  • Shuttle back to the lift, or walk if there's a line
  • No walking down the mountain. Everyone rides the chair down
  • Stay inside marked areas. Wander into the forest or ski runs and the wristband gets revoked, no refund

Racer PSAs

  • Return your rented transponder Sunday. MyLaps TR2. Not returned = $299 charge. Bring the clip back for $5 credit
  • No mid-race refueling
  • No engine work in the pit area. Nothing touching the asphalt
  • Tires and basic maintenance: The Racers Edge (vending Thu–Sun) and EMT Racing
  • No trailers or RVs in the pit area
  • Each racer vehicle has room behind it for a 10x10 tent. Load multiple bikes per vehicle

O'Neal

Oldest motocross apparel company in America. Jim O'Neal was racing TTs and desert in the early '60s and got fed up with gear that couldn't survive the sport. In 1970 he modified a bakery delivery van and started selling parts and apparel out of it at SoCal races. That van was the first office and the first warehouse. Fifty-five years later the office sits a few miles from the old Hopetown track where it started. Jim still rides, still races Baja, and holds the most class wins in Baja history.

  • Products: helmets, jerseys, pants, boots, gloves, goggles, protection. Moto, MTB, adventure
  • Free custom-printed hat booth · multiple colors, multiple logos. One of the most popular stops on the row
  • MTB side: Greg Minnaar, most successful downhill racer ever, has been on O'Neal for years
  • Confirm current moto rider roster with the rep on site
